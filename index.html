Save the Nottingham Mathematics Department

This page collects thoughts from academics, postgraduates and undergraduates, about the University of Nottingham's short-sighted proposal to cut 1 in 7 academic jobs in the mathematics department.

Submit your thoughts Read submissions

Purpose

We are collecting public statements from people who want to share their perspective on the University of Nottingham's disastrous proposal to cut 1 in 7 academic jobs in the mathematics department. Not only is this short-sighted, it also seriously casts doubt about the quality of education that can be provided to undergraduates and postgraduates.

To submit, use the form above. Please only submit something you are comfortable having published.
